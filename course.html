Visual Sketching for Designers

Learn to think on paper: quick thumbnails, clean wireframes and the confident line work that makes every later design step faster.

What you’ll learn

  • Draw straight lines, boxes and ellipses with confidence
  • Thumbnail ten layout ideas in ten minutes
  • Turn a messy sketch into a clean wireframe
  • Annotate screens so developers understand them
  • Sketch user flows and storyboards
  • Present sketches to clients without apologising

Before you start

No drawing experience needed. Bring a pencil, a black fine-liner and plain paper; a tablet is optional.
