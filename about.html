What we believe

Three promises to every student

  1. 01

    Make, don’t memorise

    Every module ends with a small project you can show someone. Quizzes are optional; making things isn’t.

  2. 02

    A human in the loop

    Your projects are reviewed by a mentor, not an algorithm, with notes you can actually act on.

  3. 03

    Fair and forever

    Buy once, keep for life, get every update. Prices adjust for your country, and refunds take one email.
