How learning works here

Four steps from curious to confident. Most students finish their first course in three weeks.

  1. 1

    Pick a course

    Filter by topic, level and time. Every course has free preview lessons.

  2. 2

    Learn in short lessons

    Videos run 6–12 minutes, with a small project at the end of each module.

  3. 3

    Get mentor feedback

    Post your project and a mentor replies with notes within two working days.

  4. 4

    Earn your certificate

    Finish the final project to get a shareable certificate for your CV or LinkedIn.

Who we are

A small school with big-hearted mentors

Lernwick started as a weekend design class in a Manchester café. Today it is a team of 40 mentors teaching in 90 countries, and we still keep classes small enough that someone knows your name.

  • Practical first. Every module ends with something you make, not a quiz.
  • Real mentors. Working professionals, reviewed by students every term.
  • Yours for life. Buy a course once and keep every future update.
